# Verified-mode semantic consumer v1 (unactivated)

This consumer owns V-mode Verifier requests and candidate-local decisions. It requires its own exact pinned algorithm in addition to the existing semantic context, request, decision and policy contracts. Its admission owner must validate the actual selected base, immutable transaction inputs, complete applicable registration chain and current work eligibility. This component does not provide that admission owner or qualify a shipping runtime mode.

The existing proposal-only journal and request entry points retain their mode guard. Explicit verified-mode entry points require `editing_mode = verified_promotion`; no P descriptor or final P decision is converted. The consumer shares durable request persistence, two-attempt/one-shape-retry allowances, retained output recovery and canonical decision/event/pointer commit mechanics with the existing semantic implementation.

The initial deterministic decision is nonfinal/unverified on pass and final/rejected on failure. Neither permits promotion. Only a complete durable semantic result can append the final second decision. The exact initial predecessor must reproduce; its timestamp is retained in its hash. A semantic pass in V mode yields `CANDIDATE_VERIFIED`, `decision_final = true` and `promotion_permitted = true`; rejection or an operator question yields a final rejected decision with no promotion permission. Final decisions never reopen. Technical failure consumes its existing attempt allowance without inventing a final semantic decision.

Request receipt completion does not commit a candidate decision. Candidate-local decision commit does not advance `rounds/current_verified.json`, publish promotion artifacts, rewrite seed/current draft bytes or imply scheduler completion. Reentry reproduces durable evidence and repeats the established publication barriers without invoking another client. Status and inspection do not repair anything.

The admission owner must provide read-only historical transaction resolution before this consumer participates in committed promotion-lineage validation. The V semantic reader recognizes only the canonical fourth disposition event after a committed, final second decision with deterministic and semantic pass. It requires the event to extend the exact verified event and directly resolves its hash-bound canonical intent, matching registration, final verification pointer and expected predecessor snapshot. Unknown events, incomplete semantic decisions and conflicting bindings refuse. The P semantic reader continues to refuse a fourth event.

Recognizing this tail does not make promotion effective: the semantic view remains candidate-local, and only the separate global lineage owner may derive promoted disposition from committed pointer ancestry. A prepared event alone neither advances authority nor reopens verification. Semantic commit reentry with a validated tail reads the existing final decision without publishing anything. The promotion owner still must prove historical registration/semantic evidence, reconcile the sole next-ordinal claimant and snapshot, and commit under the storage fence. V-mode terminal/scheduler projections remain responsibilities of that integration; no proposal-only terminal report may imply V-mode completion.

Qualification must include actual V-mode registration and independent semantic request/decision journeys, failure and recovery prefixes, unchanged global authority, mode/contract refusal, and the unchanged P-mode regressions. Historical promoted-base replay, fenced promotion, both reconciliation stages and complete activation remain later required gates.
