# Verified Phase 2 final finding reconciliation

Separately pinned extension of the bounded declaration closeout owner. Require a
consumed final scheduled v3 review of the current verified pointer. Replay original
entry/configuration, declaration, request, raw result, consumption and all source
reviews. No root mirror, cache, model claim or operator shortcut confers authority.

Reproduce latest-per-profile current-pointer issues. Persist every residual issue,
including minor/nit and out-of-scope findings, in an immutable unresolved-issue
snapshot; bind its hash and every source consumption into a reconciliation commit.
Never overwrite the issue file originally supplied to the Reviewer. Preserve full
feedback through its original result reference. Reconciliation consumes no review
credit and grants neither an Editor/candidate action nor another Reviewer request.

Closeout is eligible only for zero serious findings, no rubric/gap signal, no
authority/determinism/invariant signal, no observed feedback oscillation and complete
current-profile cleanliness. Minor/nit residuals without those signals are retained
as nonblocking evidence. Mixed serious source/declaration findings route to source
revision first; declaration-only serious findings route to declaration revision.
Authority and rubric signals have explicit blocking routes. These routes describe
required work; this owner cannot perform those repairs or emit a failure terminal.
Do not infer completed repair from issue_type, evidence prose or a hash-mismatch
label. Immutable reviewed declarations already reproduce the proven current data.

Read-only status never publishes. Fenced reconciliation repeats source barriers,
publishes residual issues before its commit, and ends after publication/recovery.
Missing committed dependencies, changed sources or conflicting bytes refuse.
Closeout on this extension requires committed reconciliation even for an empty
final review. Its v2 closeout commit binds reconciliation and residual issue refs;
ordinary acceptance checks, closeout publication and separate state recovery remain
required. Counts must still match the reviewed declaration and target matrix.

Old suites retain their original closeout behavior and artifacts. This extension
does not implement declaration repair, gap resolution, source correction from a
final review, further review scheduling, complete halt precedence, failure reports,
public activation or external apply-back. Preserve remaining ordinary review budget
without adding a pass or declaring unsupported work exhausted.
