and promotion owners. Other profiles cannot be selected. Review counts survive
promotion; cleanliness does not. Pending requests keep their existing two-slot
logical-request allowance and require explicit continuation. Complete retained

cannot be bypassed through review, focused completion or technical continuation.

FOCUSED_PROFILE_STABLE. Exhaustion without current clean evidence produces
PHASE_1_SWEEP_COMPLETE_WITH_RESIDUALS and a focused technical failure report.

configuration containing only the selected profile. Full Phase 1 prerequisites
and Phase 2 entry remain blocked. Neither focused receipt nor mutable status is
authority to expand the selected profile, budget or configured roles.

source durability barriers, ownership checks and recovery-only behavior. Missing
or changed committed dependencies fail closed. No source apply-back is permitted.
