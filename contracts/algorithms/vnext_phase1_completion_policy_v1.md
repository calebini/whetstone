# scope-aware-phase1-v1

This additive, separately pinned contract selects the shared Phase 1 policy in
`review_configuration_v3` and emits `vnext_verified_phase1_closeout_v5`.
For that configuration only, it supersedes Standard-only selection and the raw
major/blocker acceptance rule in the retained review-sequence, focused, vertical,
status, closeout, budget, review-request and command algorithms. All unchanged
identity, evidence, durability, promotion and recovery requirements still apply.

Approved all-deferred scope surfaces plus consistent canonical/advisory evidence
may defer a finding. Bare reviewer scope flags cannot. Decisions, authority/scope
conflicts and blocking invariant violations override deferral. Major/blocker
findings otherwise block, independently of workflow and profile. The scheduler,
status and closeout share this interpretation. Minor optional observations remain
nonblocking unless a decision or protected obligation applies. Dependency
conflicts require explicit scope/operator advice; prose is not deterministically
interpreted. Repeated contradictory issue advice cannot authorize deferral.

Full completion requires clean current-pointer reviews from every required
profile. Focused stability cannot establish full-phase acceptance. Closeout v5
records approved deferrals and their scope bindings; no exhausted budget grants
acceptance. New-policy Phase 2 is unavailable. Existing configurations replay
unchanged. Capability validation admits Standard/MVP and the three declared
profile sets; MVP requires approved scope. The schema/suite and every selected
profile must be validated before execution; registration alone cannot qualify a
production release.
