# Verified promotion v1 (unactivated)

This separately pinned consumer owns single-host local-filesystem promotion under the shared D8 fence. It requires genuine verified-history replay and the verified-materialization contract. It does not activate a shipping suite, invoke models, change editing authority or imply scheduler convergence/apply-back eligibility.

Read-only inspection and mutation use two ordered stages. First freshly prove committed lineage and classify its derived mirrors. Missing/stale mirrors select materialization recovery only. A mutating invocation releases its promotion critical section, calls the separate materialization-only recovery against fresh current authority, and stops. It does not inspect the next ordinal in that invocation.

Only consistent Stage 1 state permits full registration and candidate-decision replay. Registration orphans require separate registration recovery. Derive next ordinal solely as current ordinal plus one. Inspect that exact canonical intent path for every registered candidate in ascending creation order and the single next snapshot path. Neither caller choice nor timestamps select a candidate.

Exactly one valid prepared claimant reserves the next ordinal, including after later candidate registration. It must remain unpromoted, final semantically verified, with an exact current transaction-base reference, current pointer hash/ordinal, registration/map and final-decision binding. Multiple claims and every non-prefix intent/event/snapshot combination refuse. A snapshot without its matching intent and event cannot be overwritten. Earlier unbound intents are not enumerated or selected; directly inspected disposition sources still require validation.

Without a claimant or next snapshot, only the latest registered candidate may prepare. If it is unverified, rejected, already promoted or based on a different pointer, stop without fallback. Prepared semantic decisions require their own recovery. A candidate's semantic view does not by itself establish whether it was globally promoted; use proven pointer history.

Construct an immutable intent, fourth promotion disposition event and next pointer from the locked validated inputs. This version uses the intent's prepared_at timestamp for event recorded_at and pointer promoted_at too, making recovery byte-identical. Existing preparation retains its timestamp, identities and all bytes. Validate closed schemas, canonical locators, event/predecessor identity and every promotion cross-binding. No caller may supply replacement fields.

Before publishing an intent, repeat existing-byte durability barriers for the selected creation marker, final verification snapshot/current pointer and current global snapshot/pointer. Producer protocols durably publish dependencies before these commit markers; genuine replay still validates their original Editor/Verifier and candidate evidence. Synchronization creates no missing evidence and rewrites no authority.

Publish or byte-identically reuse intent, event and next snapshot in that order. Conditionally replace current_verified.json using the exact previously observed bytes and next snapshot. Inside the transition validator freshly prove current lineage, recheck materialization, replay the full registry/semantic evidence and next-ordinal reconciliation, and require the identical claimant/package. Hold the same fence continuously through this conditional commit or failure. Lock loss, stale base or uncertain acknowledgement never permits rollback or allocating another ordinal.

After successful commit, release the promotion fence and perform separate current-authority materialization repair. Report the pointer committed by this invocation separately from the actual current pointer returned after repair: another cooperating operation may have advanced authority in between. Restart after commit repairs missing mirrors and stops; already materialized reentry cannot promote the same candidate again.

Qualification requires genuine owner-driven promotion, no fallback, reserved older claimant recovery, competing and non-prefix claims, every publication/commit/materialization interruption, lock loss and competing processes, multiple promotions, same-round follow-up, read-only status and unchanged client counts. D8 audit/reconciliation, live V work/operator admission, scheduler integration and full release qualification remain required before activation.
