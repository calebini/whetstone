# Explicit draft-bound Phase 2 rubric-gap acceptance v1

Internal opt-in extension to standard target/rubric handling. The operator
ratified this policy on 2026-09-27: only an explicit operator decision can accept
an exact gap for its bound rubric, permissive target and current verified draft.
Draft/lineage changes require renewed acceptance. After a promotion, renewal
uses the original consumed review plus its committed verified-resolution receipt
and the new current pointer, before the next declaration/request is prepared. Reviewer/Editor output cannot
grant acceptance. Ordinary source findings and invariant/authority violations
remain independent blockers. No authoritative specification is amended here.

The fixed builtin rubric has no mid requirements; this owner accepts only
final/permissive gaps. Each consumed pass/current lineage may publish one immutable decision
containing explicit selected gaps, actor, rationale and time. It binds the job,
configuration, pointer, draft bytes, rubric, request, consumption, exact gap
content and requirement hashes. Read-only context and preview select nothing.
Resolved, unknown, duplicate or altered selections refuse. The decision never
edits a candidate, resolves a source finding or consumes review budget.

Publication replays those sources under the D8 fence. It occurs before any
successor request/declaration, advice/candidate work or dependent terminal/signal
report. Existing exact decisions recover without new work; divergent decisions
cannot replace them. All consumers reproduce the decision from its consumed
source prefix. Later requests hash-bind prior decision references, and declaration
v3 explicitly binds their history. Removing/changing committed evidence fails
replay. Acceptance cannot rewrite a previously reviewed declaration: changed
acceptance evidence requires an applicable remaining ordinary declaration review.
No new budget, unchanged-disagreement override or freeze release is granted.

Accepted entries retain their gap identity, evidence and status in gap artifacts;
they are nonblocking only for their exact current draft and target. Changed gap
content cannot inherit acceptance. Verified resolution still wins over acceptance.
After a draft change old receipts remain history and unresolved gaps block again.
Failure summaries exclude only accepted nonblocking gaps, preserving source debt.
Finding-reconciliation v2 and internal bound-rubric manifests v2 bind the exact
decision references in reports as well, including reports without a successor
request. Declaration/report publishers repeat their decision durability barriers.
Publication loss/ownership loss recovers exact bytes without clients or authority
advancement. Frozen suites and legacy/bridge behavior remain unchanged.
